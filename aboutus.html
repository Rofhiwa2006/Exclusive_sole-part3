OUR STORY

About Exclusive Sole

Built from a passion for sneakers, streetwear and self-expression.

Who we are

Exclusive Sole was started in Sandton, South Africa, with a simple goal: make it easier for local sneaker enthusiasts to discover sought-after styles while having greater confidence in what they buy.

Our promise

We pay attention to the details. Our sneaker inspection process considers labels, stitching, materials and other product features. We aim to provide helpful service and clear information throughout the enquiry process.

Why choose us?

  • Careful product inspection
  • Help with sourcing selected sneaker styles
  • Friendly customer support
  • A passion for sneaker culture and individuality

THE FOUNDER

Rofhiwa Theophilus Tshimomola

Founder & Owner | Exclusive Sole

Exclusive Sole was built from a passion for sneakers, streetwear and the vision to create something that represents more than just fashion. I wanted to build a brand where style meets identity — a place where every pair tells a story and every customer feels confident in what they wear.
